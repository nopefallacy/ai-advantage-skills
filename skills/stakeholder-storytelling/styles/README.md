# Optional styles

Choose a style after understanding the stakeholder and seeing any user-supplied references. These presets support the story; they do not decide its content.

| Style | Character | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Editorial Ownership](editorial-ownership/STYLE.md) | Spacious editorial typography, tactile halftone diagrams, fine geometry, warm light and deep-green dark treatments | Approved visual direction from the workshop's prototype review |

Each preset owns its concrete visual rules and reusable assets. General advice about diagram selection, source evidence, and presentation formats stays in `references/`.

The user can replace these presets with a screenshot, existing deck, written direction, or another available style skill. Approval of a preset's appearance does not make its example business content a rule for future work. New directions should be tested before being described as approved.
